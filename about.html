The Person Behind It

About Donghua Central.

Hey, it's me, Shridhuu.

I'm 19-ish years old.

I use AI tools sometimes, along with manual translation. I also use novels and wikis as references to ensure names and terms are translated correctly (sometimes I'm lazy).

I'll be making subtitles and uploading them here.

Release Schedule

Most releases will be uploaded on the same day the raw episodes are released. There may occasionally be a delay of 1–2 days due to school and other commitments (I'm in high school).

You can follow the posts to get notified as soon as I release new subtitles.

Currently Working On

Note: Beyond Time's Gaze releases will take longer since I only work on it in my spare time — I'm still about halfway through Episode 1. New episodes will usually go up the same day as the raws, or the day after.

More series will be added here as I pick up new shows.

I don't care what you think or what you have to say. I'll keep uploading donghua for people to enjoy. You can fuck off with your opinions, I don't give a shit.
