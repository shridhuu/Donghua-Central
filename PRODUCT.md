# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Chinese animation (donghua) fans, cultivation novel readers, community members tracking weekly episode releases, collectors looking for high-accuracy handcrafted English subtitles, and Nine Heavens Discord RPG players.

## Product Purpose
Deliver an authentic, immersive community destination that showcases handcrafted subtitles, provides instant real-time episode schedule tracking in local timezones, catalogs ongoing and completed series, and drives Discord community engagement and Nine Heavens RPG ascension.

## Brand Personality
Atmospheric, artisanal, reverent, authoritative. The site feels like stepping into an ancient immortal sect scripture pavilion — mystical yet functionally sharp, never cheap or gimmicky.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS templates (white/sand card grids, tracked tiny all-caps kickers on every block, soft 16px blur ghost cards).
- Pirate streaming clone clutter (popups, flashing badges, messy neon banners).
- Saturated AI tropes (side-stripe 3px accent borders, meaningless gradient text, identical 3-card feature columns).

## Design Principles
- Proof of Craft: Show subtitle quality directly with side-by-side typesetting comparisons rather than making unverified claims.
- Cultivation Atmosphere: Every surface reflects xianxia mysticism through ink, ethereal spirit glow, and calligraphic/serif contrast without sacrificing readability.
- Immediate Utility: Schedule and catalog tools must load instantly, update in local user time, filter smoothly, and require zero cognitive friction.
- Tactile Feedback: Interactive elements (switches, filters, copy buttons, ladder rungs) must have distinct active, pressed, and focus states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Target WCAG 2.1 AA compliance across all pages.
- Contrast ratio >= 4.5:1 for body and caption text against dark obsidian backdrops (fixing current low-contrast muted text).
- Full keyboard navigation and visible focus rings on search inputs, filter chips, day tabs, and cards.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion with crossfade alternatives for all animations.
