Donator Perks

Supporter Role

  • Receive the Supporter Role for approximately 1 month.
  • Includes 1 month of access to all Donator Perks.

Exclusive Content

  • Access to our exclusive supporter-only content channel.
  • Get access to content shared specifically for supporters.

Translation Requests

  • Donators who contribute $8.99 or more can request a translation for any show of their choice.
  • Translation requests are limited to up to 26 episodes per request.
  • Please keep in mind that larger requests take time, so this limit helps prevent the workload from becoming unmanageable.

Supporter Discussions

  • Access to the Supporter-only discussion channel.
  • A place for supporters to discuss shows, translations, upcoming projects, and more.

Every donation is appreciated, regardless of the amount. Your support helps us keep the server running and continue providing translations and content for everyone.
