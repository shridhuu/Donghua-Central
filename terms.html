Redistribution Rules

Terms of Republishing

Disclaimer: these terms govern redistribution of releases published under the Donghua Central tag only. Copyright in the original video, audio, and any third-party assets remains with their respective owners — nothing here changes that.

General Rules

  1. You must credit Donghua Central as the original release group — in the release description, embedded metadata, or another reasonably visible location.
  2. You must not remove, strip, or alter our credit tags from the subtitle files themselves.
  3. You must not sell, paywall, or otherwise charge for access to our releases. They're made for the community, for free — standard ad-supported hosting is fine, as long as access itself isn't paid and the site doesn't rely on deceptive or excessive advertising.
  4. You must not present our work as your own, or pass off a modified version as an official Donghua Central release.
  5. You must not hotlink directly to our servers or download links — please re-host anything you redistribute.
  6. As a courtesy, please wait at least 24 hours after our initial release before republishing elsewhere.

If You Modify Our Work

If you substantially re-time, re-translate, or re-edit our subtitles rather than redistributing them as-is:

  • Drop the Donghua Central tag from the filename.
  • Update the internal subtitle credits to reflect your changes.
  • Credit Donghua Central's release as your base source.

Recognized Redistribution Sites

No sites are specifically endorsed at this time.

These terms exist to keep releases free, correctly credited, and not misrepresented. If you have a use case not covered here, ask on Discord before assuming — see the Join Discord link.
